📱 Automatic Device Backup
Every change is copied to a second spot on this phone, replacing the old copy. Nothing is sent online. If the app's saved data goes missing, it restores itself from this copy.

Family Sync
Share your roster with your partner in real time.
⚠ Sync is the only feature that stores your data online: your roster, swaps, claims, leave and contacts are saved under your sync PIN so your partner can see them. Anyone who knows or guesses the PIN can see it too — use a long PIN that's hard to guess (8+ characters, letters and numbers).

About & disclaimer

Shift Roster is a free, personal tool made by a train driver. It is not made, checked or endorsed by Queensland Rail or any union.

  • Your posted roster, the Roster Office, your payslip and the Enterprise Agreement always take priority over anything the app shows.
  • Pay figures are estimates. Rules are a plain-English summary and can be incomplete or out of date. The Time check uses typical walk, prep and run times — actual times can differ.
  • Check anything important with the Roster Office, your manager or your delegate before relying on it.
  • Keep your own backups. The app is provided as is and you use it at your own risk; the developer is not responsible for missed shifts, pay errors, lost data or any other loss.
